    How it works in your Jira

    1. Write it down onceThe person who knows the fix saves the known error with its error codes and workaround steps.
    2. It shows up in the ticketWhen a new incident matches, the app suggests it right in the incident and shows why it matches.
    3. One clickAny agent clicks Apply workaround and the steps land as an internal comment, even if they never saw that error before.

    Good to know

    • Works on every Jira Service Management Cloud plan, including Standard.
    • Built on Atlassian Forge. Your data stays in your Atlassian site.
    • No AI. Every suggestion shows the reason it matches.
    • Comes with 40 example known errors so you can test it in minutes.
