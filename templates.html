Seven rules for known errors that agents can find

Agents find a known error only when it uses the user's words and the exact error code.

  1. Write the title the way users describe the problem, not the way IT names the cause.
  2. Copy error codes exactly, with prefixes such as 0x or AADSTS.
  3. Write one known error per cause. When two causes show the same message, write two and fill in Not this one if.
  4. Add at least three Also described as lines taken from real incidents.
  5. Write the workaround so a new agent can follow it alone, and test it once before you publish it.
  6. Give every known error an owner and a review date. When the cause is fixed, set it to Retired instead of deleting it.
  7. Link each incident you solve with it. The counts show which problems to fix for good first.

Post-incident review template

Hold a review within 5 working days of every major incident, for example one that hit many users or stopped a service for more than an hour.

Describe what the systems and steps allowed, never who is to blame, so people share what really happened.

SectionWhat to write
SummaryWhat happened, in 2 or 3 sentences
ImpactWho was affected, how many users, for how long, which services
Timeline (UTC)When it started, was detected, acknowledged, mitigated and resolved
DetectionHow you found out, and how you could find out sooner
Root causeThe chain of causes, for example from asking why five times
What went wellSteps that limited the damage
What went badlySteps that slowed the fix
Known errorsKnown errors created or updated, with links
Follow-upDate to check that every action item is done

Use your known errors inside Jira

Known Errors & Workarounds is an app for Jira Service Management that brings these templates into Jira. It is in free beta now and goes on the Atlassian Marketplace in January.

  • It keeps your known errors in Jira with the core fields from this pack: title, error codes, service, also described as, workaround, state, owner and review date.
  • When a new incident matches a known error, the app suggests it right in the incident and shows why it matches.
  • Any agent adds the workaround with one click, as an internal note or as a reply to the customer.
  • It runs on Atlassian Forge, so your data stays inside your Atlassian site. It works on every Jira Service Management Cloud plan, including Standard.
